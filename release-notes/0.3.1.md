## What's new in 0.3.1

The card flow in the chat now shows the gears with the AI recommendation and your choice side by side, and a Claude run waiting on a background task no longer ends too early.

### Cards and the flow

- The flow card in the chat and the complexity card beside it show the gears, the AI recommendation in blue and your choice in green, with the table of the six dimensions.
- Each assessment keeps the limits it was scored with, so older versions still read the same after the limits change.

### Agents

- When a Claude background task gives no sign, GearMod checks whether its command is still running and keeps waiting. If nothing is left, it ends the run and says why in the chat. After 30 minutes of silence, it asks the agent in the chat how its tasks are going.

### Upgrading

GearMod 0.3.0 finds this version by itself and offers to update. Your projects, cards and settings are kept.
